Tips for a Great Lesson

  • Be specific — general advice is less helpful than a real story
  • Focus on what you learned, not just what happened
  • Keep your lesson actionable so others can apply it immediately
  • Vulnerability is strength — honest stories get the most helpful marks
  • Post anonymously if you're sharing something personal
